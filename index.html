Bet Guide
AUTOSAVE: Your bankroll, bets, point, odds, Come/Don’t Come, ATS progress, roll history, streaks, and settings save automatically on this device. Reopening the app restores the session. Use NEW GAME to erase it.
ON / OFF PUCK: The small dealer puck shows OFF before a point is established. After the come-out roll sets a point, it flips to ON and moves onto that point number. When the point is made or a 7 ends the hand, it returns to OFF.
CHIP CONTROLS: Quick tap an existing chip to add the currently selected denomination to that bet. Press and hold for about 0.65 seconds to pick the chip up, then drag it to another compatible bet or swipe it down to REMOVE.
CHIP STACKS: Bets now use casino-style denomination colors on the felt: $1 white, $5 red, $10 blue, $25 green, $100 black, and $500 purple. Mixed bets display as a physical stack instead of one generic black chip.
MOVE CHIPS: Press and hold an existing chip until it lifts under your finger. Drag it to another compatible betting area to move that amount. Swipe/drag it down to the red REMOVE zone to take it off the table and return it to your bankroll. Invalid drops snap back.
PHYSICAL DICE can be turned ON or OFF. ON uses the longer casino-style tumble/bounce presentation. OFF uses the original quick dice. The random result is generated the same way in either mode.
